For every room

  • Check-in from 13:00, check-out by 10:00. On arrival day you can park and leave luggage from 8:00; on departure day until 19:00.
  • Children aged 3 to 11 get 40 % off; children under 3 without their own bed stay free. We lend a cot.
  • Breakfast 180 CZK, children 90 CZK.
  • A dog up to 10 kg or a cat is 200 CZK a night, a dog over 10 kg 400 CZK. We lend bowls.
  • Cancelling or changing less than 24 hours before arrival (by 13:00) costs the price of the first night.
  • The city tourist tax for adults is added. No smoking in the rooms or in the wellness suites.
  • Wi-Fi, parking on the grounds under camera watch, coffee and tea are included. For longer stays and several rooms we offer our own price.
