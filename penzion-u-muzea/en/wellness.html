Private wellness

Three suites in our villa, always just for you. For 90 or 120 minutes, or the whole night from 19:00 to 10:00.

How long

De luxe

On the 1st floor. A Finnish sauna with salt and aroma therapy and a long whirlpool. You rest in a separate part that looks like a bedroom: a big bed, parquet, clay plaster and a winter garden with flowers.

With a fireplace

On the ground floor. A large bathroom with a round whirlpool that looks out onto the fire. A double bed, TV and a sofa corner. There is no sauna in this one.

Lower-ground suite

The smallest of the three. A Finnish sauna, a round whirlpool and a 140 cm bed right next to the tub.
