Kobido

Japonská masáž obličeje Kobido v tichém bytě u parku Rajská zahrada. Jeden masér, jedno lehátko, jen na objednání.

Kobido je stará japonská masáž obličeje: pracuje s pletí, svaly i lymfou a nekončí u tváří – patří k ní i hlava, krk, ramena a dekolt.

Kobido, the Japanese facial massage, in a quiet flat by Rajská zahrada park. One practitioner, one table, by appointment only.

Kobido is an old Japanese facial massage: it works the skin, the muscles and the lymph, and it doesn't stop at the cheeks — the scalp, neck, shoulders and décolleté are part of it.

Postupně dojde na obličej, hlavu, krk, ramena i dekolt.

Odcházíte s prokrvenou, pevnější pletí a hlavou, která má na chvíli klid.

We start slowly. I cleanse the skin, then alternate slow strokes with quicker ones — Kobido is half rest, half work.

In turn the face, scalp, neck, shoulders and décolleté all get their time.

You leave with skin that is flushed and firmer, and a head that is quiet for a while.

Facial massage isn't right for active acne, allergy-prone or irritated skin. If you're not sure, message me before you book — better to settle it in advance than on the table.
